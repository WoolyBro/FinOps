An AI billing-operations agent for freelancers, built on the Strands Agents SDK.

“Rahul paid me ₹15,000 today.”
The problem

Every freelancer is also their own accounts department.

Scattered

Three apps, one ledger

Invoices in a spreadsheet, payments in a banking app, reminders in WhatsApp.

Constant

Small work that never stops

Raise invoices, match each transfer to the right invoice, chase what’s late.

Costly

Mistakes that erode trust

A payment on the wrong invoice. A reminder for money already received.

Who it’s for

Independent freelancers and small studios, without a bookkeeper.

Designers, developers, writers, consultants

People billing a handful of clients a month and doing the admin themselves.

Built for India first

Rupees and lakhs, Indian digit grouping (₹1,50,000.00), UPI and NEFT payments.

In their own words

“40k”, “1.5 lakh”, “Rahul paid me today”: no forms required, though every form still works.

Why it matters

AI can talk about your money. It shouldn’t be trusted to invent it.

A chat assistant

Confidently gives a balance it never looked up

Fluent, plausible and unverifiable. Wrong numbers look exactly like right ones.

FreelanceFlow

Every number comes from a tool reading the ledger

The agent shows each step it ran, and refuses rather than guesses.

The AI decides what to do. Deterministic code does everything that touches money.

Built to be trusted

The guarantees are in the code, not the prompt.

  • 593 automated tests, with no credentials needed
  • Overpayments refused with the real outstanding balance
  • Duplicates flagged, and the agent must ask before a second one
  • Result cards built from tool output, not model text
  • Gapless invoice numbers assigned by the database
  • Keys and server paths never reach the browser
